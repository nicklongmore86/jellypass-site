Free and open source · for Jellyfin and Seerr

One Jellyfin server. A private space for every household.

JellyPass sits between your households and Jellyfin. Each household gets its own address, sees only its own members at sign-in, and asks for new titles through Seerr without anyone else learning what they asked for. Jellyfin stays in charge of playback; JellyPass keeps the households apart.

Get started View on GitHub

What it does

  • Household privacy on one Jellyfin

    Titles a household asks for are tagged private in Jellyfin and blocked for everyone outside that household, using Jellyfin's own blocked-tag policies. Administrators still see everything.

  • Private requests through Seerr

    Members tap Get this. JellyPass makes the Seerr request as that member, and when it arrives only their household can see it. Nobody learns what another household asked for, or that they asked.

  • A front door per household

    Each household gets its own address, such as https://jelly-grove.example.com. Its sign-in screen lists only its own members, with one-tap sign-in for members without a password.

  • Phone, web and TV

    An app for iPhone, iPad and Android, an installable web app on every household address, and an optional add-on that brings Discover rows and requests into Jellyfin's TV interface.

  • Admin tools

    A web admin site, signed in with your Jellyfin administrator account: households, members, grants, bulk access for existing titles, request approval and a catalogue of your library.

  • Nothing phones home

    JellyPass runs on your own server and sends nothing to its developer. No analytics, no accounts with us. MIT licensed.

Who it is for

I run a Jellyfin server

JellyPass runs in Docker next to Jellyfin and Seerr. You need Jellyfin 10.11 or 12, Seerr, a reverse proxy with HTTPS, and a domain for household addresses.

Install JellyPass · Check the prerequisites

Someone gave me a JellyPass address

Enter it in the JellyPass app, or open it in your browser and add /jellypass/app/. Sign in with your Jellyfin name and password.
