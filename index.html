What is JellyPass?

JellyPass gives each household on a shared Jellyfin server its own private space.

  • Your household's library. Browse and search titles, and see at a glance what's ready to watch.
  • Ask for something new. Tap Get this to ask your household's administrator to add a title, then follow it in My requests.
  • Private between households. Other households can't see what you've asked for, and you can't see theirs.
  • Watch in Jellyfin. Play opens the title in your household's own Jellyfin.

JellyPass works with a JellyPass household server, which runs next to Jellyfin and Seerr. If someone has given you a JellyPass address (it looks like https://jelly-yourhousehold.example.com), enter it in the app to get started.

Running your own Jellyfin? JellyPass is open source: github.com/nicklongmore86/jellypass.

JellyPass is unofficial and not affiliated with Jellyfin or Seerr. Film and series data from TMDB; streaming availability from JustWatch.
