JellyPass privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

JellyPass is an unofficial companion app for a JellyPass household server, which runs alongside a self-hosted Jellyfin media server. It is not affiliated with Jellyfin or Seerr.

The short version

  • The developer of JellyPass collects nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and it never sends anything to the developer.
  • Everything the app sends goes only to the household server address you enter, apart from loading film and series images from TMDB (see below). That server is run by your household's administrator (the person who runs your Jellyfin), not by the developer of this app.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your household server address, your Jellyfin sign-in token, your user ID and your display name. These are kept in the iPhone/iPad Keychain or Android's encrypted secure storage, readable only by this app (on iPhone and iPad, only while the device is unlocked), and they are not synced or moved to another device; Android backup is turned off for this app.
  • A random device identifier, created once, so your household's Jellyfin can list this device.
  • Signing out deletes the stored sign-in.

What the app sends, and where

All of the following is sent only to the household server you chose:

  • your username and password, once, when you sign in (the app does not keep your password);
  • your sign-in token with each request;
  • the device's model name, platform, app version and the random device identifier, so the server can show this device in its Devices list;
  • what you browse, search for and ask for ("Get this"), and your requests.

Posters, backdrops and logos are loaded by the app directly from TMDB's image server (image.tmdb.org). Like any image on the web, those requests show TMDB your device's IP address and which images were loaded; your sign-in token and account details are not sent with them.

The app does not use cookies.

Data held by your household server

Your account, your requests and your household's library are held by your household's server and its administrator. To see, change or delete that information, contact your household's administrator. The developer of this app has no access to it.

Third-party content

Film and series details and artwork are provided by TMDB and streaming availability by JustWatch. Details and availability are fetched by your household server; artwork is loaded from TMDB as described above. Tapping their credit links opens their websites in your browser, where their own privacy policies apply. Playing a title opens your household's own Jellyfin.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Your household's administrator controls what each account can see.

This website

This website (jellypass.app) runs no JavaScript, sets no cookies and uses no analytics; it loads nothing from any other site. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and the page you ask for in order to deliver it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
