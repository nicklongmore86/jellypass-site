Phone, web and TV apps

Members browse what's new, see what their household already has, tap Get this and follow My requests. Every app signs in with Jellyfin and sends your sign-in, searches and requests only to the household's own address; the one exception is artwork: posters, backdrops and logos load straight from TMDB's image server (image.tmdb.org), which sees your IP address and which images were loaded but no account details (see the privacy policy). All of them need the household app API switched on, which the setup wizard can do, or:

HOUSEHOLD_APP_ENABLED=true
TMDB_API_KEY=tmdb-key

with the household gateway and Seerr configured. Replace tmdb-key with your own key: JellyPass checks it at startup, and the placeholder stops it. Without the setting, the apps' paths answer 404. Each household also has a Mobile app access switch, on by default; with it off, its members get 403 app_access_disabled in every JellyPass app (see App and TV switches).

Household web app

Every household address serves an installable web app at /jellypass/app/, for example https://grove.example.com/jellypass/app/. It is the one app every member can use today. It shares its sign-in with Jellyfin web on that address, so Play opens Jellyfin already signed in. Install it from the browser: Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen, or Chrome's Install app. On iPhone and iPad, sign in inside the installed app, which has its own storage.

Nothing to configure beyond the settings above: the app is built into the image.

iPhone, iPad and Android

The phone app is not publicly available yet. It isn't on the App Store or Google Play; the App Store release is not out yet.

  • iPhone and iPad: through TestFlight, by invitation from whoever runs your server.
  • Android: an APK installed directly (sideloaded); ask whoever runs your server for the link. Android asks the first time to allow installing from your browser or file manager, and later APKs install over the old one, keeping the sign-in.
  • Server: JellyPass 3.2.0 or later with the household app on. Cancel request and the day each title was requested need 3.5.0 or later, and Not interested with the Hidden tab needs 4.0.0 or later; older servers simply don't show them.
  • Address: any HTTPS address, for example https://grove.example.com. The app refuses http://, IP addresses and .local names. App versions before 1.3.0 accept only addresses beginning jelly-.
  • Privacy: the sign-in is kept in the iOS Keychain or Android's encrypted storage. No analytics or crash reporting. See the privacy policy.

TV add-on (experimental)

The TV add-on adds JellyPass to Jellyfin web's own interface on chosen households' addresses: a Discover row on Home, a Discover row on Search, title pages with Play and Get this, My requests, and a Discover page behind a compass button in Jellyfin's header. It is experimental and off by default, and it depends on Jellyfin web's page structure, so it is matched to Jellyfin web 12.x and 10.11.x only.

Turn on the global gate, then switch on one household first with its TV add-on injection switch in Groups, and check it on your TV before adding others. Other households are unchanged. Members must add the household address (not your LAN address or main Jellyfin address) as their server.

TV_ADDON_ENABLED=true

The easiest first check is a desktop browser on the household address, where the same row appears in Jellyfin web. From 4.1.0, on a TV the add-on also lowers Jellyfin web's own search to at most 100 items and 24 people, studios or artists.

Samsung TVs (advanced, unsupported)

Jellyfin's Samsung (Tizen) app bundles its own copy of Jellyfin web, so the add-on can't reach it. The repository has a JellyPass build of that app (tv/samsung/) that loads the add-on from the household address. You build it yourself. It replaces the Jellyfin app on the TV, needs the TV in Developer Mode and Samsung's sdb tool, and uninstalling the stock app first clears its servers and sign-ins. It is aimed at people comfortable sideloading TV apps, comes with no support, and needs a rebuild when your Jellyfin web version changes; see the TV add-on design notes.
